ARCADE
NEON QUEST

Reco as an after-hours arcade. The wallet is a high-score scoreboard, tasks are missions, badges are tournament trophies. Aimed at the kid who finds "kid apps" cringe and wants something that feels just slightly forbidden.

// CONCEPT 01 NOTES //

STRONGEST WEAKNESS:

The arcade-neon aesthetic skews boy-coded and "edgy." Kids 9-11 split sharply on whether dark, neon, gamified screens read as cool or as overstimulating. If either Lia or Yael leans away from arcade-game aesthetics in real life, this concept will feel like a costume they don't want to wear. Also: the dark theme makes evidence-photo previews and reward thumbnails harder to read at a glance — kids' eyes have to work harder for what should be an effortless interface.

Quietest strength: the high-score-display framing makes the wallet ledger feel like an objective record, not a parent-controlled allowance. Coins become "points the system minted" rather than "money mom gave me," which matches the actual ledger semantics — and the streak/total campaign visuals (chain + scoreboard) are the most intuitive of the three concepts.
