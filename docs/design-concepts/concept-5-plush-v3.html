— concept 05 v3 notes —

The icon split, explicitly

▲ KEPT (v2)
  • · Fox + bunny character avatars
  • · Task icons (bed, tooth, food, shirt, camera)
  • · Gold coin icon (already in the right family)
  • · Tab bar line icons (utility, quiet)
  • · Status pills, progress bars, layout primitives
★ UPGRADED (v3)
  • · 8 badge emblems → flat-illustrated multi-color
  • · 6 reward icons → flat-illustrated reward-pack style
  • · Shield inner panel → light pastel (was dark)
  • · Ribbon banner color → tints to badge's primary color
  • · Sibling-earned chip → uses the same upgraded emblems

The principle is "spend illustration budget where the kid is staring." Tasks and avatars are part of daily flow — they should feel familiar and fast. Badges and rewards are destinations — the kid wants to look at them, show them, plan toward them. Those earn the production-grade flat-illustrated treatment.
