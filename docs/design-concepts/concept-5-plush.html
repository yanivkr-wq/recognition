Concept 05 — Reco design deck · Child Reward-inspired

Plush

Inspired by Child Reward's signature 3D metallic shield badges, cartoon-character avatars, and soft pastel surfaces. Whitespace-rich and friendly. Pink primary CTAs. Vertical task timelines. The "premium kid app" aesthetic — polished illustration work doing the heavy lifting. Heart of the system: the shield collection as the kid's living trophy case.

— concept 05 notes —

Strongest weakness

The signature element — the 3D metallic shield badge — only pays off if every badge gets real illustration work behind it. Eight starter badges × multiple emblem expressions × locked-state variants × per-year-birthday rebrands adds up to a real ongoing illustration budget. Without that investment, the shields become generic and the whole concept reads as "Child Reward but without the production value." This concept has the highest illustration debt of the five.

Quietest strength: this is the only concept that treats the badge as the hero asset. Family_Tasks_Hub's badges are utility; Spark's badges are decoration; here they're collectible objects with their own gravity. For an app whose core long-term motivation is "earn cool badges over the course of a year," that's a strategic match — and the kids who pick avatar characters (a bear, a fox) get a sustained identity that competes naturally with the sibling on the next device.
