— CONCEPT 02 NOTES —

STRONGEST WEAKNESS

The hero-dossier framing leans hard into a fantasy register that some 9–11 year olds will find irresistible and others will find embarrassing. Reading words like "Trove" and "Decree" in English (or "מגילה" / "צו" if we Hebrew-ize them harder) every day risks the same fate as Habitica's medieval-RPG mode — beloved by a niche, opt-out for everyone else. The narrative voice in margin annotations needs careful copywriting; bad fantasy copy is worse than no fantasy copy.

Quietest strength: this concept makes attribution beautiful. Every joker action gets a wax seal. Every approval gets a gold seal. The ledger reads like a chronicle. The narrative wrapper accidentally turns the "mandatory reason on admin actions" feature from a bureaucratic friction into a story beat. And the audit feed has natural visual variety (different seals, different ribbons) that a flat list of "[event] at [time]" doesn't have.
