Concept 04 — Reco design deck · Duolingo-inspired

Spark

Coiny

Duolingo's playbook adapted for chores. Chunky 3D buttons with a satisfying bottom ledge. A mascot, Coiny, with expressions. Saturated brand colors stacked confidently. League-style leaderboard for sibling competition. Streak flames + lesson paths + crown collections. Built for the kid who already knows how Duolingo feels in her thumbs.

— concept 04 notes —

Strongest weakness

Inheriting Duolingo's visual vocabulary is a fast way to "feels like the kid already uses it" but also a fast way to "feels like a knock-off." Two specific risks: (1) the 3D-button-with-ledge pattern is now strongly trademarked-feeling — kids who play Duolingo daily will notice and may treat Reco as "Duo's cousin," which is fine until the brand wants its own identity; (2) Coiny the mascot needs real illustration work to compete with Duo, not just an SVG smiley face. The mascot is the make-or-break here.

Quietest strength: this concept already speaks the kids' UX language. Lia and Yael (presumably) know what a chunky green button does, what a streak flame means, what a weekly league looks like. Reco doesn't need to teach the genre — it just plugs into a vocabulary they already have. And the league framing — "this week, Yael is #1, you're #2" — converts the abstract "wallet difference" into a concrete weekly competition that resets, which avoids the demoralization risk of a perpetual leaderboard.
